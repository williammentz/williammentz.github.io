I am currently a Statistical Science graduate student at Duke University. My academic and professional experiences have equipped me with strong analytical and leadership skills, helping me approach problems from multiple angles and uncover insights beyond the surface.

My current research interests include exploring the intersection of algorithmic and statistical methods and symbolic music in the [AI Music and Creativity Lab](https://ai-music-and-creativity-lab.github.io/). I am actively developing methods to algorithmically reduce polyphonic (multi-lined) music into monophonic (single-lined) music.
